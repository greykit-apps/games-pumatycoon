# PumaTycoon

**Produce, sell into a market you and your rivals keep moving, and outbid them for the railroads.** A trading game for 2–5 players: against bots, against friends sharing one screen, or any mix of the two.

**Play:** [greykit.com/games/pumatycoon](https://greykit.com/games/pumatycoon/)

![A four-player game in progress: the market across the top, the standings beside it, your hand of cards below, the railroads, town and buildings beneath, and your cash and actions on the right](screenshots/table.png)

## How it plays

On your turn you take one action: **produce** by playing a card, which gives you commodities and pushes prices up; **sell** one commodity, which drops its price $1 a unit; **auction** a railroad; **buy** or upgrade a building; or **build a town**, paid for in commodities. Every producer pushes prices up and every seller pushes them down, so the market is yours to move, and everyone else's.

The game ends when the last town is built or the last railroad is auctioned, and the round is played out. Points come from towns, railroad lines, buildings, town and railroad pairs, and an optional secret mission. The most points wins; a tie goes to whoever has more money. Every choice shows what it would do before you commit, and anything that spends takes a second press. Bots play at Easy, Normal or Hard, and optional rules include missions, sudden death and a beginners' building set.

## Keys

| Key | |
|---|---|
| `1`–`5` | preview producing with that card |
| `S` | jump to Sell |
| `A` `B` `T` | jump to the railroads, buildings or town |
| `Enter` | commit, take the keyboard, or bid |
| `P` | pass in an auction |
| `?` | rules, scoring and every key |

Everything else is a button.

## Saves

The game saves after every move, in the browser's `localStorage` under keys starting `pumatycoon_`. **Settings → Forget everything** clears them.
